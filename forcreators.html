For Creators

Scripts are listed only if the name of their creator is registered. To add your name to the database, enter it above.

The scripts are updated automatically every 24 hours. Changes you make will not be immediately visible.

To add tags to your scripts, insert the following element anywherein the script's description on NumWorks:

<div class="pynumstore-tags" title="tag1,tag2,tag3"></div>

PyNumStore will automatically detect and index the tags. You can see a live example on NumWorks and on PyNumStore.
